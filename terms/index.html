Who we are

Permits Clerk is a private document-preparation and filing-assistance service. We are not a government agency, and we are not a law firm — we don't provide legal advice. Every state lets you apply for a seller's permit directly with its own agency, usually for free or a small state fee; what you pay us for is preparation, error-checking, and handling the filing for you.

The service

Our services start at $75. The full-service seller's permit filing offered through our application flow costs $179 and includes application preparation, a check for common errors, submission to the relevant state agency, and delivery of your confirmation and documents by email. Any fee the state itself charges is separate from our service fee and is disclosed on your state's information page.

We typically begin processing within one business day of payment. State agencies control their own processing times, which vary by state.
